[Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) project
by Google, distributed under the Apache License 2.0.

The icons have been minimally modified from the upstream source: a `fill="#cccccc"`
attribute has been injected on each `<path>` element so the icons render
visibly on the application's dark sidebar/toolbar.

The full Apache 2.0 license text is available at:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
